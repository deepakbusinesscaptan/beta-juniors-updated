Our Program

Beta Juniors Age-Based Learning Programs

Mother Toddler Program

1–2 Years

A gentle start for little learners, focused on sensory development, bonding activities, and early social interaction.

Toddler & Creative Program

2–3 Years

Encourages curiosity, creativity, and communication skills through play-based and activity-driven learning.

Nursery / Junior KG

3–4 Years
